What you are seeing

A vibrating plate contains regions that move strongly and nodal lines that barely move. Loose grains are repeatedly thrown away from the strongly moving regions and collect along those nodal lines.

The classic buttons use idealised square-plate mode equations. Live sound estimates the dominant pitch and loudness, then uses them to excite and blend nearby virtual modes.
